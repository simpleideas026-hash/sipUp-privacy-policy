Delete Your SipUp Account

Last updated: 14 Aug 2026

You can permanently delete your SipUp account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how, what gets deleted, and how long it takes.

Option 1 — Delete it directly in the app (fastest)

  1. Open SipUp and make sure you're signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the Me tab → My Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account near the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Deletion happens immediately — your account and all associated data are removed right away, with no waiting period.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app installed

If you no longer have SipUp installed, or can't sign in, email simpleideas026@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my SipUp account." Include the email address you signed up with. We'll process the request within 7 business days and confirm once it's done.

What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account itself (email, name)
  • Your hydration profile (weight, activity level, wake/sleep schedule, goals)
  • Your entire hydration history
  • Your reminder settings and preferences

If you submitted feedback through the app before deleting your account, that feedback record is not automatically deleted, since it's not linked back to your account once submitted. Contact simpleideas026@gmail.comif you'd like a specific feedback submission removed too.

Using SipUp Without an Account

If you'd rather not have any data stored on our servers at all, you can use SipUp's Guest mode instead of creating an account — all data stays on your device only, and is removed automatically when you uninstall the app. Nothing to delete on our end, since nothing was ever sent to us.
